March 15, 2026 — In Effect
2026-03-15
● In Effect
Maximum TLS Certificate Validity: 200 Days
First phase of the phased reduction from 398 days to 47 days. CAs must not issue subscriber certificates with validity exceeding 200 days.
 Validity SC-081v3 ↗ Ballot
2026-03-15
● In Effect
DCV Reuse Period Reduced to 200 Days
Maximum period for reusing domain control validation data drops from 398 days to 200 days.
 DCV / Data Reuse SC-081v3 ↗ Ballot
2026-03-15
● In Effect
Subject Identity Information (SII) Reuse: 398 Days
Organizational identity data reuse for OV/EV certificates drops from 825 days to 398 days. Requires more frequent re-verification of business credentials.
 DCV / Data Reuse SC-081v3 ↗ Ballot
2026-03-15
● In Effect
Short-Lived Certificate Threshold: 7 Days
Definition of "Short-Lived Subscriber Certificate" tightens from ≤10 days to ≤7 days validity.
 Validity SC-081v3 ↗ TLS BRs
2026-03-15
● In Effect
DNSSEC Validation Required for CAA & DCV Lookups
CAs must validate DNSSEC (when present) back to the IANA root trust anchor on all DNS queries for CAA record lookups and domain control validation by the Primary Network Perspective. Delegation of this check is prohibited.
 DNSSEC SC-085v2 ↗ Ballot
2026-03-15
● In Effect
SHA-1 Signatures Prohibited in Certificates & CRLs
All remaining use of SHA-1 in certificate and CRL signatures is prohibited. Unexpired Subordinate CA certificates with SHA-1 signatures must be revoked.
 Crypto / SHA-1 SC-097 ↗ Ballot
2026-03-15
● In Effect
Precertificate Signing CAs Prohibited
CAs must not issue new certificates using the Precertificate Signing CA profile (Section 7.1.2.4), and existing Precertificate Signing CAs must not be used to issue new precertificates.
 Infrastructure SC-092 ↗ Ballot
2026-03-15
● In Effect
Email/Crossover DCV Methods: SHOULD NOT Use
Email-based DCV methods (3.2.2.4.4, .13, .14) transition to SHOULD NOT. Method 3.2.2.4.8 (IP Address "crossover" method) is hard prohibited as of this date.
 DCV / Data Reuse SC-090 ↗ Ballot
June 15, 2026 — Upcoming
2026-06-15
◐ Upcoming
MPIC: Increase to 4 Remote Network Perspectives
Phased rollout of Multi-Perspective Issuance Corroboration raises the minimum number of remote network perspectives used for domain validation and CAA checks to 4.
 MPIC SC-067v3 ↗ Ballot
December 15, 2026 — Upcoming
2026-12-15
◐ Upcoming
MPIC: Increase to 5 Remote Network Perspectives
Final phase of the MPIC phased rollout. Quorum requirements increase to require 5 or more geographically diverse remote perspectives for all domain validation and CAA checks.
 MPIC SC-067v3 ↗ Ballot
March 15, 2027 — Future
2027-03-15
○ Future
Maximum TLS Certificate Validity: 100 Days
Second phase of the phased validity reduction. Manual certificate management becomes practically unsustainable at this threshold.
 Validity SC-081v3 ↗ Ballot
2027-03-15
○ Future
DCV Reuse Period Reduced to 100 Days
Domain control validation reuse drops from 200 days to 100 days. Parallel to the validity reduction.
 DCV / Data Reuse SC-081v3 ↗ Ballot
2027-03-15
○ Future
Email, Phone & Fax DCV Methods: Hard Prohibited
All remaining email (3.2.2.4.4, .13, .14), phone (3.2.2.4.16, .17, 3.2.2.5.5), fax/SMS (3.2.2.5.2) domain validation methods are fully prohibited. Prior validations using these methods may not be reused.
 DCV / Data Reuse SC-090 ↗ Ballot
March 15, 2029 — Future
2029-03-15
○ Future
Maximum TLS Certificate Validity: 47 Days
Final phase. Certificates must not exceed 47 days validity. Represents an ~8× increase in renewal frequency vs. the 398-day era. Automation is mandatory at this stage.
 Validity SC-081v3 ↗ Ballot
2029-03-15
○ Future
DCV Reuse Period Reduced to 10 Days
Domain control validation data may only be reused for 10 days, making near-continuous automated domain validation effectively mandatory.
 DCV / Data Reuse SC-081v3 ↗ Ballot
